# Public-Copy

PC-1 Identify audience, genre and established facts. Organize value introductions around outcomes, mechanisms and practical benefits so readers can understand the current solution independently.

PC-2 Explain value through concrete capabilities and mechanisms. Express rationale as goals and benefits. Quantified benefits need evidence; label unverified benefits as expected.

PC-3 Value introductions contain outcomes and benefits. Place problem costs, historical comparisons, negative consequences and risk analysis in sections explicitly requested or required by the genre. State conditions for accurate use as clear applicability. Preserve material facts without disguising them.

PC-4 Use what was delivered, how it works and what value it provides as review questions, adapting structure to the genre. Keep reader-relevant mechanisms; remove internal drafting history, author self-certification and discarded-option explanations.

PC-5 Generate titles, filenames, summaries, captions and metadata from current results. Commits and PRs describe current behavior, including triggering conditions, compatibility effects and verification when needed. Preserve facts required by quotations, audits and migration notes.

PC-6 Review prose and packaging for facts, audience needs, incremental information and consistency with the observed state.
